IS-Net general-use model and ONNX export

The model weights originate from DIS by Xuebin Qin, Hang Dai, Xiaobin Hu,
Deng-Ping Fan, Ling Shao, and Luc Van Gool, and are identified for this
project as Apache-2.0. See Apache-2.0.txt.

The approved fp32 ONNX export is distributed by rembg under MIT. See
rembg-MIT.txt. The fp16 model served by this project was locally converted
from the hash-verified rembg fp32 file; auxiliary outputs after the first
matte output were removed. The conversion and source hashes are recorded in
docs/models.md.
